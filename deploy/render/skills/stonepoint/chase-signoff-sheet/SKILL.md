# Chase a missing sign-off sheet

Use when the owner needs to ask a store manager, technician or client contact for a signed work-completion sheet, photos or an approval that is holding up an invoice.

- Pull the job with `ops_portal` to get the WO/PO number, site and date of service.
- Draft a 3-5 sentence message in the owner's voice (signs as Michael Shaer, Managing Member, Stonepoint Property Services LLC): one line of context (job, site, date), the exact document needed, how to send it (reply with a photo of the signed sheet is fine), and a thank-you. No pressure language, no legal threats.
- Offer a text version (under 300 characters) and an email version. Do not send anything yourself unless the owner explicitly says to send it.
